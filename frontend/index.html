How It Works

Three steps to resolution

From submission to resolution — our AI handles the routing so you don't have to navigate government bureaucracy.

1

Submit Your Complaint

Describe the issue, add your location, and attach photos or videos as evidence. Takes less than 2 minutes.

2

AI Classifies & Routes

Our ML model instantly reads your complaint and assigns it to the correct city department with a priority score.

3

Track & Get Resolved

Follow your complaint status from Registered → In Progress → Resolved directly from your dashboard.

Features

Everything you need

A complete platform for citizens and administrators to manage civic complaints efficiently.

🤖

ML Classification

BERT-powered model auto-assigns every complaint to the right department with high accuracy — no manual sorting needed.

📍

Location-Aware Priority

GPS coordinates combined with area density scoring ensures high-impact complaints are flagged as top priority.

📷

Photo & Video Evidence

Attach up to 5 photos and 3 videos directly to your complaint so admins get the full picture immediately.

🛡️

Role-Based Access

Citizens, department admins, and superadmins each see exactly what they need — nothing more, nothing less.

📊

Real-Time Dashboard

Admins can sort, filter, and update complaint statuses in real time with a clean, scrollable dashboard.

⚡

Instant Status Tracking

Citizens get live updates as their complaint moves from Registered to In Progress to Resolved.
